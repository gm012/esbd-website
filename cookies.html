ESBD · WEBSITE INFORMATION

Cookies and preferences

Control optional map content

Essential form session

When you submit an enquiry, the esbd_enquiry session cookie helps validate the request and prevent forgery. It is restricted to the form endpoint and expires with your browser session. It is not an advertising cookie.

Your saved choice

The esbd_preferences_v1 entry in browser local storage records whether you allow Google Maps and the time of that choice. It expires after six months. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies only during the current page visit.

Optional Google Maps

No embedded map is requested until you allow maps. Google may receive connection information and use its own cookies when its map loads. Rejecting maps leaves the rest of the site and enquiry form available. The separate “open map” link takes you to Google when you choose to follow it.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer to allow or reject maps at any time. Rejecting removes the embedded map and prevents future loads. It cannot remove cookies already set on Google’s domain; use your browser controls for those. A supported Global Privacy Control signal keeps optional maps disabled.

Analytics

This version does not install analytics or advertising tags. Any future tracking integration must be reviewed and added to these controls before use.
