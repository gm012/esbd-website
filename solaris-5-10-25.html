Solaris / 5, 10 & 25

Thermal protection. Built in paper.

Solaris 5, 10 and 25 use flexible, paper-based materials with reflective and insulating properties to protect temperature-sensitive palletised goods against fluctuations during transit. Three protection levels. One recyclable thermal system.

01 / Paper-Based Thermal Covers

Protect the temperature. Protect the cargo.

Solaris 5, 10 and 25 form a thermal barrier around palletised goods using advanced paper-based materials with reflective and insulating properties.

Beyond thermal performance, the construction is tear resistant and water resistant — adding another layer of protection against the hazards that cargo can face throughout transportation and handling.

03 / Choose Your Protection Level

Three levels. One paper-based system.

Solaris 5, 10 and 25 use the same core paper-based approach with different insulation depths and thermal performance. Select the level that matches the duration and temperature exposure of your logistics operation.
