Pallet Porter / Reusable

Build the second level. Use it again.

Single Pallet Porter – Reusable is a reinforced HDPE and polypropylene system designed to protect palletised goods while creating a secure second cargo level above them. Built for repeated use and closed distribution loops, each unit can support up to 700kg above the original load.

01 / What It Does

Protect below. Carry more above.

Pallet Porter is assembled around an existing palletised load to form a lightweight but strong supporting structure.

Once the protected pallet is forklifted into the truck or container, another pallet can be loaded onto the structure above it. That becomes your second cargo level — without placing the additional weight directly onto the goods underneath.

02 / 2nd Level Capacity

Up to 700kg. Above every unit.

Each reusable Single Pallet Porter is capable of supporting up to 700kg on its second level. This allows otherwise unused vertical trailer or container space to become productive payload capacity.
