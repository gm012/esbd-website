Temperature Protection / Solaris

Protect the load. Lose the plastic.

Solaris protects temperature-sensitive palletised goods with a paper-based thermal barrier designed as an eco-conscious alternative to conventional plastic and foil thermal covers — without compromising cargo protection.

01 / What is Solaris?

Thermal protection. Built beyond temperature.

Solaris creates a protected environment around temperature-sensitive palletised goods while also helping shield cargo from the wider risks that can appear during transit.

Constructed purely from paper, the system combines a white reflective external coating with micro-convective air-pocket insulation to deliver thermal performance, physical protection and a fully recyclable alternative to conventional plastic and foil covers.

02 / The System

Engineered in paper. Built to perform.

Solaris combines specialist paper engineering with reflective and insulating layers to protect temperature-sensitive cargo through demanding transport conditions.

Stringent testing has demonstrated thermal performance comparable with conventional plastic thermal covers — providing a recyclable alternative without giving up the protection your cargo needs.

03 / Solaris Range

The right cover. For the right journey.

Solaris 5, 10 and 25 provide progressively higher levels of paper-based thermal insulation, while Solaris S20 adds structural stacking capability for operations that also need to maximise freight cube.

04 / Why Solaris?

Reduce the plastic. Keep the protection.

Solaris is designed to offer the thermal performance required for temperature-sensitive freight while replacing conventional plastic and foil cover materials with a recyclable paper-based system.

The result is a solution that protects cargo, supports plastic reduction goals and — with Solaris S20 — can also unlock more freight capacity through pallet stacking across land, sea and air.

Reliable Thermal Protection Tested performance for demanding hot and cold stress conditions.
100% Recyclable Paper Insulation Paper-only construction provides a simpler end-of-life pathway.
Reduce Plastic Replace conventional plastic and foil thermal covers with paper-based protection.
Protect Cargo Help shield palletised goods from thermal exposure and transit hazards.
Maximise Cube Solaris S20 enables pallet stacking with loads up to 500kg.
