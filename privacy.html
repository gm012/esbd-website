Privacy notice

How this website handles your information

Who to contact

ES Business Development (PTY) Ltd (ESBD) operates this website. For privacy questions or requests, email info@esbd.co.za or call +27 72 261 9873. Our published postal address is PO Box 291, Boschenmeer, 7646, Cape Town, South Africa.

Enquiries

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional company and message. We use these details to respond to your request and discuss relevant packaging solutions. Required fields are needed to process the enquiry. Please do not include sensitive personal information. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Hosting and security

Our hosting and email providers process information needed to operate the website and deliver enquiries. Server logs may include your IP address, browser information and request time. The form uses a short-lived session and temporary keyed network-address digests to help prevent abuse. Enquiry messages are sent to our business mailbox; the website does not keep a separate message database.

External services

Google Fonts and the Font Awesome stylesheet from Cloudflare CDN are requested to display the approved design. These providers receive connection information such as your IP address. Google Maps is loaded only if you allow optional maps. External providers may process data outside South Africa under their own privacy policies. External links, including LinkedIn and Google Maps links, open services with their own practices.

Retention and your choices

Website rate-limit files expire after approximately two hours and are cleaned on subsequent form use. Saved map preferences expire after six months. Enquiries in our mailbox and hosting logs follow the applicable business and provider retention arrangements; contact us for details. You may ask to access or correct your information, request deletion where applicable, or object to processing. We may need to verify your identity. You can also contact the South African Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Updates

This notice describes the website configuration dated 6 October 2026. It should be reviewed when services or business practices change.
