Solaris / S20

Thermal protection. Built to stack.

Solaris S20 combines the thermal protection of the Solaris range with structural strength that allows pallets to be stacked. Protect temperature-sensitive cargo, maximise available cube and unlock more freight capacity from the same transport space.

01 / Stackable Thermal Protection

Protect the load. Then build above it.

Solaris S20 provides the paper-based thermal protection of the Solaris range while adding a rigid structural shell around the pallet.

Constructed from corrugated, water-resistant cardboard laminated to insulating embossed paper, S20 is designed to withstand freight handling, impacts and crushing while supporting an additional load above the protected pallet.

02 / Structural Protection

One protected pallet. Another level above it.

The structural strength of Solaris S20 allows another palletised load to be positioned above the protected goods. This turns unused vertical space into usable freight capacity without placing the weight of the upper load directly onto the cargo below.

03 / Why Solaris S20?

Thermal protection. Structural efficiency.

S20 is designed for cold-chain operations that need more than a thermal cover. It combines recyclable paper-based insulation with a rigid protective structure that helps safeguard cargo and make better use of available freight space.
