Pallet Porter / Recyclable

Build the second level. Recycle it when you're done.

Single Pallet Porter – Recyclable is built from heavy-duty corrugated cardboard to protect palletised goods while creating a secure second cargo level above them. Designed for single or limited reuse, it is suited to long-haul distribution where returning the system to the warehouse is not practical or financially viable.

01 / What It Does

Protect below. Carry more above.

The recyclable Pallet Porter forms a heavy-duty corrugated sleeve around an existing palletised load.

Once the top load pad is fitted, the structure creates a secure second level capable of supporting another pallet above. The lower goods stay protected while unused vertical freight space becomes usable payload capacity.

02 / Built for Long-Haul

When return logistics don't make sense.

Not every freight route returns to its point of origin. Single Pallet Porter – Recyclable is designed for single or limited reuse, making it suited to long-haul operations where returning a reusable system would not be practical or financially viable.

03 / 2nd Level Capacity

Up to 500kg. Above every unit.

Once assembled around the palletised load and fitted with its top load pad, each recyclable Pallet Porter can support up to 500kg on the second level.

Create the 2nd Level The top load pad creates a supporting platform above the goods.

05 / Simple 3-Step Assembly

Flat packed to freight ready. In three moves.

01

Open the Sleeve

Open out the flat-packed corrugated Pallet Porter sleeve ready to receive the palletised load.

02

Place Over the Pallet

Position the open sleeve around the palletised goods to create the protective structure.

03

Fit, Load & Stack

Fit the top load pad, forklift the assembled unit into the truck or container and place the next pallet above.

Repeat the process down the length of the trailer or container.
